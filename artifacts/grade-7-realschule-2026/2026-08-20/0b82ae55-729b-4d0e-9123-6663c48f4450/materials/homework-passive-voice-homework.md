## Part A — Transform (5 sentences)

Rewrite each sentence in the passive voice. You do not need to say who does the action.

1. They broadcast the show every evening.
   → _______________________________________________

2. The director films the scenes in a studio.
   → _______________________________________________

3. They publish the magazine every week.
   → _______________________________________________

4. The editor edits the script carefully.
   → _______________________________________________

5. They perform the songs live.
   → _______________________________________________

**Answer key (check your work):**
1. The show is broadcast every evening.
2. The scenes are filmed in a studio.
3. The magazine is published every week.
4. The script is edited carefully.
5. The songs are performed live.
