Speaking Practice: Free Conversations (Band 3)

Cut out the cards. Each pair draws one card. Use the conversation phrases you have learned -- no word bank, no frames. Speak freely.

You must use: one opener • at least one continuer • one closer

Phrase types to use:
Opener • Continuer (interest / clarification / repetition) • Closer

Card A

Situation: You meet a classmate on the first day back at school.

  • Find out what they did in summer.
  • Tell them one thing you did.
  • End the conversation politely.

Card B

Situation: You are waiting for the lesson to start and don't know the person next to you.

  • Introduce yourself and start a conversation.
  • Ask what subjects they have this year.
  • Say you have to go when the teacher arrives.

Card C

Situation: Your friend says something you don't understand.

  • Ask them to explain or repeat.
  • React with interest when you understand.
  • End the conversation because you have to go to your next lesson.

Card D

Situation: You want to ask a classmate about the homework.

  • Start the conversation politely.
  • Ask your question and show you are listening.
  • Thank them and close the conversation.
