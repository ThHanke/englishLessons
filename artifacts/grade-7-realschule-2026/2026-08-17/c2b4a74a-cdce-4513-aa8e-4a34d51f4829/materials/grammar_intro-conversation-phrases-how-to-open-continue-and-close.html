Conversation phrases: how to open, continue, and close

In English conversations, we use fixed phrases to start, keep going, and end a conversation politely. These phrases are like building blocks -- you do not invent them each time, you learn and reuse them. There are three types: openers (how you start), continuers (how you show interest and keep the conversation going), and closers (how you end politely). Note: in German you might say "Tschuss" to end a conversation -- in English we use fuller phrases like "See you later!" or "It was nice talking to you." Also watch out: "handy" in English means useful or convenient -- it does NOT mean mobile phone (Handy).

  • Opener (informal): meeting a classmate
    Hey, did you have a good summer? / Hi, can I ask you something?
  • Opener (more formal): approaching someone you don't know well
    Excuse me, do you know when the lesson starts?
  • Continuer: showing interest
    Really? Tell me more! / That sounds great!
  • Continuer: asking for clarification
    What do you mean exactly? / Sorry, can you say that again?
  • Closer: ending the conversation
    OK, I have to go. See you later! / Thanks for chatting. Bye!
