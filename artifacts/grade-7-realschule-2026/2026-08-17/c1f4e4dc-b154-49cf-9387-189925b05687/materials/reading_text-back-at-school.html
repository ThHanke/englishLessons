Back at School

Emma walks into the classroom on the first day back. The room is cleaned every morning by the school caretaker. New books are given to every student. Timetables are explained by the form teacher.

"Hi! Long time no see!" says Emma to her friend Ben. "How are you doing?" Ben smiles. "Great, thanks! What do you think about our new teacher?" "Really? Tell me more!" Emma laughs. "Anyway, I have to go now. Talk later! See you!"
