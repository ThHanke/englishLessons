Homework — Passive Voice & Conversation Phrases

Class: Grade 7 | Due: next lesson | [fk.g.passive, fk.w.open_continue_close_conversations]

Remember: Passive = is / are + past participle
Example: The teacher explains the lesson. → The lesson is explained by the teacher.

Part 1 — Active to Passive (5 points)

Rewrite each sentence in the passive. No word bank — try from memory!

  1. The caretaker cleans the toilets every day.
    →
  2. The headteacher reads the school rules at assembly.
    →
  3. Teachers give students their timetables in the first week.
    →
  4. The school uses computers in every classroom.
    →
  5. Students write the date at the top of every page.
    →

Part 2 — Write a Dialogue (5 points)

Write a short dialogue (6–8 lines) between two students meeting after the summer holidays. Use at least 3 phrases — one to open, one to continue, one to close.

Phrases from class:
Open: Hi! Long time no see! • How are you doing? • What’s up?
Continue: Really? Tell me more! • What do you think about...? • That’s interesting!
Close: Anyway, I have to go. • Talk later! See you!

Write your dialogue here...

Answer key (Part 1): 1. The toilets are cleaned every day.   2. The school rules are read at assembly.   3. Students are given their timetables in the first week.   4. Computers are used in every classroom.   5. The date is written at the top of every page.
