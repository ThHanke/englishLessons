Simple Past — regular -ed and key irregular verbs

To talk about finished actions in the past, we use the Simple Past. For most verbs, add -ed to the end: play → played, watch → watched, clean → cleaned. Some very common verbs are irregular — they change completely and you need to learn them: go → went, be → was / were, have → had, do → did. Important: In German, you often say "Ich habe gespielt" (Perfekt) when talking about the past. In English, we say "I played" — no extra helping verb needed. This is the Simple Past. For negatives, use "did not" (short form: didn't) + the base verb: "I didn't go to school." For questions, use "Did" at the start: "Did you watch TV?" — and use the base verb, not the past form.

  • I play football every day.
    Yesterday I played football.
  • She watches TV every evening.
    Last night she watched TV.
  • He goes to school.
    Yesterday he went to school.
  • We are happy.
    We were happy.
  • I don't go to school.
    I didn't go to school yesterday.
