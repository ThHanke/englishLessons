A Day in Ben's Life

Ben is twelve years old. He lives in London.

On Monday, Ben gets up at seven o'clock. In the morning, he has breakfast at home. He goes to school at half past seven.

At school, Ben has lunch at twelve o'clock. In the afternoon, he plays football with his friends.

In the evening, Ben does his homework at six o'clock. He watches TV at eight o'clock. He goes to bed at half past nine.
