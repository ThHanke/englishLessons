Simple Past — finished actions (recap)

Use Simple Past to talk about things that are finished and over — yesterday, last week, this morning. Regular verbs: add -ed to base form. walk → walked, watch → watched. Six irregular forms to know: go → went, have → had, do → did, eat → ate, see → saw, be → was/were. Important: In German you say "Ich habe gegessen" — two words. In English: "I ate" — one word, no "have". Do NOT add "have" when telling a story about the past.

  • Ich habe Fruhstuck gegessen. (German Perfekt)
    I ate breakfast. (English Simple Past — one word, no have)
  • She go to school yesterday.
    She went to school yesterday.
  • He have lunch at twelve.
    He had lunch at twelve.
  • They do their homework after school.
    They did their homework after school.
