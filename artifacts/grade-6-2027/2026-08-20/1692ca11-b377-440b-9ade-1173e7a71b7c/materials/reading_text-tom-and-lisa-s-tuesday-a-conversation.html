Tom and Lisa's Tuesday — a conversation

Tom: Hey Lisa! How was your Tuesday? Lisa: Pretty good! I woke up at half past six. Then I had breakfast with my mum.

Tom: What did you eat? Lisa: I ate toast and a banana. What about you?

Tom: I got up late — at seven. I only had a glass of milk. Lisa: Oh! Did you go to school by bus?

Tom: Yes, I took the bus. It was cold in the morning. Did you walk? Lisa: Yes, I walked. It was OK.

Tom: What did you do at lunchtime? Lisa: I ate a sandwich in the canteen. And you?

Tom: I went home for lunch. My dad made soup. Lisa: Nice! And after school?

Tom: I did my homework first. Then I watched TV for an hour. Lisa: I went to football practice. We played really well!

Tom: Cool! And in the evening? Lisa: I read a book and went to bed at nine.

Tom: I went to bed at half past nine. I was really tired.
