# Homework: My Yesterday

Write **5 sentences** about YOUR yesterday using **Simple Past**.

Use at least **3 different time expressions** from the box:

> in the morning | at lunchtime | after school | in the evening | at ... o'clock

---

**Sentence starters (use if needed):**

- In the morning, I ...
- At lunchtime, I ...
- After school, I ...
- In the evening, I ...
- I went to bed at ...

---

**Checklist before handing in:**

- [ ] Every verb in Simple Past (one word — "I ate", not "I have eaten")
- [ ] Subject before verb ("Yesterday I went" — not "Yesterday went I")
- [ ] At least 3 different time expressions
- [ ] 5 sentences total

---

*Example (do not copy):*
> In the morning, I woke up at seven o'clock. At lunchtime, I ate a sandwich. After school, I did my homework. In the evening, I watched TV. I went to bed at half past nine.
