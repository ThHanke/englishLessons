# Homework: My Yesterday

Write **5 sentences** about YOUR yesterday using the **Simple Past**.

Use at least **3 different time expressions** from the box:

> in the morning &nbsp;|&nbsp; at lunchtime &nbsp;|&nbsp; after school &nbsp;|&nbsp; in the evening &nbsp;|&nbsp; at ... o'clock

---

**Sentence starters (use these if you need help):**

- In the morning, I ...
- At lunchtime, I ...
- After school, I ...
- In the evening, I ...
- I went to bed at ...

---

**Checklist before you hand in:**

- [ ] Every sentence uses Simple Past (one word — not *I have eaten*, but *I ate*)
- [ ] Subject comes before the verb (*Yesterday I went* — not *Yesterday went I*)
- [ ] At least 3 different time expressions used
- [ ] 5 sentences written

---

*Example (do not copy — write about your own day):*
> In the morning, I woke up at seven o'clock. At lunchtime, I ate a sandwich. After school, I did my homework. In the evening, I watched TV. I went to bed at half past nine.
