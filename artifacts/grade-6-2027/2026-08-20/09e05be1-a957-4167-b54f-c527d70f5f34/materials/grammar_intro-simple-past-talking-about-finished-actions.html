Simple Past — talking about finished actions

Use Simple Past for finished actions in the past — things that happened and are done. Regular verbs: add -ed to base form. walk → walked, arrive → arrived, watch → watched. Irregular verbs change completely. Learn these six: go → went, have → had, do → did, eat → ate, see → saw, be → was (I/he/she/it) or were (we/you/they). Important for German speakers: German says "Ich habe gegessen" (two words) for past stories. English says "I ate" — one word, no "have". This is the biggest difference. Word order: after time words like "yesterday" or "then", subject still comes before verb. "Yesterday I went to school." Not "Yesterday went I to school."

  • I walk to school every day.
    Yesterday I walked to school.
  • She eats breakfast at home.
    She ate breakfast at seven o'clock.
  • We go to the cinema.
    Last week, we went to the cinema.
  • Ich habe gegessen. (German Perfekt for past story)
    I ate. (English Simple Past — one word, no have)
