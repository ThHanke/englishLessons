Mia's Tuesday

I woke up at seven o'clock on Tuesday morning. I had breakfast with my family. We ate toast and drank orange juice.

Then I caught the bus to school. I arrived at half past eight. At school, I had lunch with my friends. We ate sandwiches.

After school, I came home and did my homework. In the evening, my family had dinner together. We ate pasta. Later, I went to bed at nine o'clock.
