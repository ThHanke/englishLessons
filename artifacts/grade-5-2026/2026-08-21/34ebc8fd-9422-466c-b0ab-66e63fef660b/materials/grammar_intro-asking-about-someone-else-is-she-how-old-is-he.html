Asking about someone else: Is she...? / How old is he?

You already know how to say things about a family member: "She is my sister. He is 10 years old." To turn a statement into a question, you swap "he" or "she" with "is" -- "is" jumps to the front. For question words like "How old", "What", or "Where", the question word comes first, then "is", then "he" or "she". Good news: German works the same way! "Wie alt ist sie?" and "How old is she?" both flip the verb forward. The verb and the person swap places in both languages.

  • She is my sister.
    Is she your sister?
  • He is 10 years old.
    How old is he?
  • She is from England.
    Where is she from?
  • His name is Tom.
    What is his name?
