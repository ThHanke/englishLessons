He is / She is — talking about your family

You already know "am" and "is" and "are". Now we use "is" for family members. Use "he is" for a boy or man (your dad, your brother, your grandpa). Use "she is" for a girl or woman (your mum, your sister, your grandma). Good news: this works exactly like German! "Er ist" = "He is". "Sie ist" = "She is". Same idea, just different words. One important thing: in German, "sie" can mean "she" OR "they". In English, these are two different words — "she" (one girl or woman) and "they" (more than one person). Do not mix them up. Never say "He is have a sister" or "She is 8 years old has". Keep it simple: "He is 10." "She is my sister."

  • My sister → ?
    My sister is 8 years old. She is 8 years old.
  • My dad → ?
    My dad is from Germany. He is from Germany.
  • My grandma → ?
    My grandma is 65 years old. She is 65 years old.
  • This is my brother Tom. He is 12 years old. He is from Berlin.
