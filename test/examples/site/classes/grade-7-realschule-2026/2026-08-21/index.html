Back in school — who does what

Class: grade-7-realschule-2026 · Date: 2026-08-21 · Phase: new_input

Focus competences

  • fk.g.passive — active and passive voice

Lesson plan

Objectives

  • Identify active vs passive voice in context
  • Produce passive-voice sentences about school and media topics

Stages

StageDurationDescription
Warm-up / Review9 minRetrieval practice + German Passiv bridge; "I can..." on the board
Input10 minDialog with passive-voice sentences in context; underline be+past-participle
Guided Practice20 minGap fill (supported), MCQ + matching (guided)
Production5 minError correction (independent, no hints)
Wrap-up1 minExit ticket: one passive sentence about school

Differentiation

Band 1 gets a full word bank and base-verb hints; Band 2 drops the word bank; Band 3 (error correction) gets no hints at all, per B1 expectations.

Planned exercises

  • gap_fill: 3 sentences, supported band, hints given
  • mcq: 3 items, guided band, no word bank
  • matching: 5 vocab pairs, guided band
  • error_correction: 3 German L1 transfer errors, independent band
  • crossword: 4 vocab items, recall/spelling practice

Materials

  • School Life Crossword (crossword)
  • Find the Mistake: Tense & Word Order (error_correction)
  • Passive Voice: Everyday Routines (gap_fill)
  • Match the Vocabulary: School Life (matching)
  • Passive Voice: Multiple Choice (mcq)
  • New Vocabulary: Back in School (vocab_intro)
